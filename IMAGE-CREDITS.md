The featured project images are rendered excerpts of original presentation boards from Bushra's supplied Wix portfolio. Compact rasterised web editions containing every original PDF page are bundled under `public/documents/`; selected full boards and cover regions are under `public/images/projects/`. Team credits remain visible. These depict academic proposals, not completed buildings.
